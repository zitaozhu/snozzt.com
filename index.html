Sleep tracking that respects your privacy.

Snozzt records overnight, classifies what it hears on-device, and never sends a single byte of audio anywhere.

Get Snozzt Support my work How it works

Your audio stays on your phone.

Sound analysis runs in real time using Apple's on-device Neural Engine. Snozzt only stores event metadata — timestamp, what was detected, how confident the model was. No recordings. No uploads. No cloud.

What Snozzt detects, tracks, and shows you.

On-device sound classification

Snoring, sleep talking, coughs, breathing, sirens, alarms, barking — all classified locally. No round-trip to a server.

Sleep score & weekly trends

Every session gets a 0–100 score from duration, restfulness, snoring, and disruptions. Charts show how your nights compare.

Smart wake-up

A time-sensitive alarm cuts through Sleep Focus, and a chime plays in-app when you're already up. Background recording keeps going even with the screen locked.

Detailed event timeline

Each session detail shows every detected sound on a chart, colored by class, with timestamps and confidence — so you can spot what woke you and when.

Five languages, zero accounts

English, Simplified & Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean. Sign in with Apple is the only login — no email lists, no tracking pixels.

Optimized for overnight

Voice-activity detection, classifier subsampling, and tuned audio buffers keep battery drain in check across an 8-hour session.
